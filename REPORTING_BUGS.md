# Reporting bugs and translation corrections

Search [existing issues](https://github.com/Bojaingles/Bomberman-Land-2-Translation/issues) first, then [open a report](https://github.com/Bojaingles/Bomberman-Land-2-Translation/issues/new/choose).

- **Translation:** mistranslations, typos, unnatural or missing text. Quote the English line, identify its context and explain the problem. Original Japanese and a suggested correction help when available but are optional.
- **Layout:** clipped, overlapping, misaligned or badly spaced text. Include a screenshot and the menu/dialogue location.
- **Technical:** crashes, freezes, broken events, save or installation errors. Explain how to reproduce the problem, what happened and what should happen.

Always include the **exact patch version** (not “latest”), zone/location and NPC/minigame/menu. Include steps, relevant screenshots or a short clip, and whether it happens after a full restart with a normal memory-card save. For emulator issues include the Dolphin version and platform; for hardware include console, loader and version. Back up saves before testing.

For installation problems include source/output sizes and SHA-256 checksums and the exact error. Never upload a game ISO, extracted game archives, credentials or private information. Relevant screenshots and logs are enough.
