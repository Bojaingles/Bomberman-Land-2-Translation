# Known limitations

- This public test has not been verified through a complete playthrough or on a real console.
- Some choice windows, mail, attraction names and other long labels may still have scene-specific fitting problems. Please report the precise location and a screenshot.
- English manga artwork is not integrated into the playable patch. Manga title lists are translated, while comic pages remain Japanese.
- Japanese voice acting remains intact. Full speech subtitling and general movie subtitles are unfinished.
- Some text baked into illustrations, textures or movies remains Japanese.
- Old emulator save states may restore old executable code. Fully restart and load a normal memory-card save when testing a new version.

Please report additional problems using the issue forms rather than assuming they are already known.
